Description of Services

Tarbuuz.ai operates an online marketplace platform that connects event Foodies, verified Hosts, and Suppliers through a suite of purpose-built AI agents.

3.1 Platform User Roles

🍽️
Foodie (Food Enthusiasts)

Create events and approve AI-suggested menus, browse and order specialty offerings directly from Suppliers, access ingredients and prepared foods at reduced prices, support sustainability and reduce food waste, and review and rate events after completion.

🎉
Hosts (Event Hosts)

Review event requirements and custom menu proposals, submit competitive bids for event requests, host events after winning bids, and review and rate event menus after completion.

🏪
Suppliers (Event Suppliers)

List specialty offerings — cakes, tableware, décor, entertainment, and other event extras — for Foodies to browse and order directly, fulfil requests for Specialty Organic Produce & Ingredients, and review and rate event menus after completion.

3.2 Food Marketplace

Suppliers can list inventory of products for sale, manage or supply inventory, pricing, and pickup windows, and connect with consumers seeking food products.

3.3 AI-Powered Features

  • Smart Menu — generates complete, costed draft menus from a plain-language description of the occasion
  • Agentic Chef — validates the Smart Menu, flags dietary and allergen conflicts, and proposes a signature dish
  • Intelligent Bidding — ranks competing Host bids by true value: price, ratings, distance, and fit
  • Predictive Analytics — forecasts headcount, spend, and timing from patterns across past events
  • Dietary Intelligence — flags allergens and adapts dishes for vegan, halal, gluten-free, and other needs
  • Budget Optimizer — rebalances menu, staffing, and Host selection to hit a target budget
ℹ️
Platform Role: Tarbuuz.ai is a technology platform that facilitates connections between users. We are NOT a catering company or food service provider, a party to any agreements between Foodies and Hosts, responsible for the quality, safety, or legality of services provided through the Platform, or an employer of Hosts, Suppliers, or other users.
