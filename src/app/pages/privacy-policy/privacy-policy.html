Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and protect our services. The information we collect falls into three categories: information you provide to us, information we collect automatically, and information from third parties.

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

👤
Account Information

Full name, email address, phone number, encrypted password, profile photo (optional), mailing address, date of birth, and gender (optional).

🏢
Business Information

Business name and type, address and contact info, Tax ID (EIN), business license and permit numbers, professional certifications, food handler permits, and insurance certificates. (Hosts and Suppliers only.)

💳
Payment Information

Credit/debit card data (stored by third-party processors only), bank account details for direct deposit, billing address, payment history, and transaction records.

🎉
Event & Menu Data

Event type, date, time, location, guest count, dietary requirements, budget, cuisine preferences, service style, menu proposals, ingredient lists, allergen info, pricing, portfolio photos, and professional bio.

💬
Communications

Messages sent through the Platform between users, customer support inquiries, feedback and surveys, reviews you write, and emails or notifications you send or receive.

🛡️
Verification Information

Government-issued ID, Social Security Number (for background checks where applicable), background check consent and results, business licenses, health permits, insurance certificates, and professional credentials.

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device & Usage: IP address, device type and model, OS version, browser type, unique device identifiers, mobile network info, app version, language preference, and time zone settings
  • Platform Activity: Pages viewed, features used, links clicked, search queries, browsing history, bid and booking activity, transaction history, time on page, navigation paths, and error logs
  • Location Data: Approximate location from IP address; precise GPS location with your permission — used for nearby food supplier search, event location verification, delivery radius, and local ingredient recommendations
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Session cookies, analytics pixels, web beacons, local storage, and device fingerprinting — see Section 8 for full details

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Background check providers: Criminal history, sex offender registry, license verification, employment verification, and credit history (certain account types)
  • Social media platforms (if connected): Profile info, friend lists, publicly available data, and content you've shared
  • Payment processors: Payment verification status, transaction details, fraud risk assessments, and chargeback information
  • Analytics providers: Demographic information, interest categories, device usage patterns, and cross-platform behavior
  • Data enrichment services: Business verification data, address standardization, email validation, and phone number verification
  • Public sources: Business registration records, professional licensing databases, food safety inspection results, and court records (for verification purposes)

How We Use Your Information

2.1 Provide & Improve the Platform

Platform Operation

Create and manage accounts, authenticate identity, process bookings and transactions, facilitate communication between users, enable event hosting and bidding, and manage menu proposals and supplier inventory.

AI-Powered Features

Rank competing Host bids by true value, generate Smart Menu recommendations, validate menus and flag allergens with Agentic Chef, optimize pricing and budgets, suggest seasonal ingredients, and forecast headcount and spend with Predictive Analytics.

Payment Processing

Process payments and refunds, detect and prevent fraud, calculate fees and commissions, issue payouts to Hosts and Suppliers, generate tax documents (1099 forms), and maintain financial records.

Platform Improvement

Analyze usage patterns and trends, conduct research and development, test new features, troubleshoot technical issues, monitor performance, and gather user feedback.

2.2 Safety & Security

We verify your identity and credentials, conduct background checks for Hosts, validate business licenses and permits, ensure food safety compliance, detect and prevent fraudulent activity, investigate disputes, review reported users and content, and enforce our Terms of Use and policies.

2.3 Communication

We send booking confirmations, transaction receipts, account activity notices, security alerts, and Platform updates. With your consent, we also send promotional emails, feature announcements, newsletters, personalized recommendations, and referral program invitations. You may opt out of marketing at any time — see Section 6.4.

📧
Mandatory emails (cannot opt out): Booking confirmations and receipts, account security alerts, Terms or Privacy Policy changes, payment notifications, critical Platform updates, and legal notices.

2.4 Legal & Regulatory

We use your information to comply with applicable laws and regulations, respond to legal requests and court orders, enforce our Terms of Use, protect our rights and property, defend against legal claims, fulfill tax and accounting obligations, and meet food safety reporting, anti-money laundering (AML), and Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements.

2.5 Research & Analytics

We analyze how users interact with the Platform, identify popular features and content, measure adoption and conversion rates, understand culinary trends and regional food culture, study dietary restriction patterns, and evaluate pricing strategies. We also use data to improve AI recommendation algorithms, train menu validation models, and enhance matching accuracy.

All research uses aggregate, anonymized data unless you provide specific consent for identifiable use.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to provide, protect, and improve the Platform. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you, remember your preferences, understand Platform usage, deliver personalized content, and measure advertising effectiveness.

Required
Strictly Necessary

Authentication and account access, security and fraud prevention, load balancing and performance, core Platform functionality, and session management. Cannot be disabled.

Functional
Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences (language, location), cart items, and provide enhanced features and a customized experience.

Analytics
Analytics Cookies

Understand Platform usage, measure feature performance, identify popular content, track conversion rates, and generate usage statistics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel).

Advertising
Advertising Cookies

Deliver relevant ads, measure effectiveness, limit ad frequency, personalize ad content, and enable retargeting across websites (Facebook Pixel, Google Ads).

8.3 Third-Party Cookies

  • Google Analytics: Traffic analysis and usage patterns — Privacy Policy · Opt-Out
  • Facebook Pixel: Ad measurement and targeting — Privacy Policy · Opt-out via Facebook ad preferences
  • Stripe: Payment processing and fraud prevention — Privacy Policy · Necessary for transactions; cannot be disabled

8.4 Other Tracking Technologies

  • Web Beacons (Pixels): Embedded in emails to track opens, monitor campaign effectiveness, and track conversions from ads
  • Local Storage: Stores preferences locally for faster loading, offline functionality, and caching frequently used data
  • Device Fingerprinting: Combines device characteristics for fraud detection, security monitoring, and account protection

8.5 Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser: Chrome (Settings › Privacy and Security › Cookies), Firefox (Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies), Safari (Preferences › Privacy › Cookies), Edge (Settings › Privacy › Cookies). Note: blocking cookies may affect Platform functionality.

Additional opt-out tools: NAI Opt-Out · DAA Opt-Out · Google Ad Settings

We do not currently respond to Do Not Track signals as there is no industry standard for implementation; however, we honor browser cookie controls.
